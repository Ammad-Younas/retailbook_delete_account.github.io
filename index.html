Customer app: how to delete your account

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open the Retail Book customer app and log in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Enter your password (or choose your Google account if you signed in with Google) and tap Delete.

Option 2: by email

Email ammadyounas.tech@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my Retail Book account". Say whether you also want your records at shops deleted. We reply to confirm and complete the request within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Login: name, email, password or Google sign-in linkDeleted immediately.
Copy of your data stored on your phoneDeleted immediately.
Your records at the shops you joined: ledger entries, package, EasyLoad and credit requests, credit history Kept. These are business records of those shops. They are deleted when the shop owner deletes you as a customer, when the shop owner deletes their own account, or when you ask us by email to delete everything (completed within 30 days).
Your phone numberKept, linked to your old email, so that nobody else can register it as you. Deleted together with your shop records as above.

If you sign up again later with the same email, the records still kept by your shops will be shown to you again.

Shop owner app: how to delete your account

  1. Email ammadyounas.tech@gmail.com from the email address you use to log in to Retail Book.
  2. Use the subject "Delete my Retail Book account" and include your shop name and owner code (shown in the app under Settings).
  3. We reply to confirm the request came from you, then delete your account and data within 30 days.

Tip: before asking, you can share or note down any ledger you want to keep, because deleted data cannot be recovered.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Login: name, email, password or Google sign-in linkDeleted.
Shop profile, shop name and owner codeDeleted. Customers can no longer find or join your shop.
Customers you added, their ledgers, credits, requests and activation historyDeleted.
Packages, offers, cities, durations and categoriesDeleted.
Your accounts, payment accounts and their transaction historyDeleted.
Your customers' own Retail Book loginsKept. They belong to the customers; only their link to your shop is removed.
Copy stored on your phoneDeleted when you log out or uninstall the app.

Retention period

  • Deletion done in the customer app takes effect immediately.
  • Requests sent by email are completed within 30 days.
  • We keep no other copy once deletion is completed, and deleted data cannot be recovered.
  • We may keep the request email itself as proof that the request was handled.

More details are in the Retail Book privacy policy. Questions: ammadyounas.tech@gmail.com
